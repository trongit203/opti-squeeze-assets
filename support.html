Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your device. OptiSqueeze has no accounts and no server that receives your photos or PDFs. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where are my processed files saved?

On Android, results are saved to Downloads/OptiSqueeze unless you choose to overwrite the original in place. On iOS, results are saved through the system share and save options shown when processing finishes.

The compressed file is the same size as the original. Why?

OptiSqueeze never makes a file larger. If recompression would produce a bigger file, the original is kept. Try the Maximum preset for the smallest result.

Is text in my PDF still selectable after compression?

Yes. The default PDF strategy recompresses embedded images only, so fonts, text, and vectors stay intact and searchable.

I can't open a locked PDF or forgot its password.

The Unlock tool needs the correct password to remove protection. OptiSqueeze cannot recover or bypass a password you don't know.

Which formats can I convert between?

Images: HEIC, PNG, JPG, and WEBP. You can also turn images into a PDF and PDF pages back into images.

The app is slow or fails on a very large file.

Everything runs locally, so very large PDFs or high-resolution photos use more memory and time. Close other apps, process fewer files at once, or try a smaller batch. If it keeps failing, email us the details above.

How do I delete my data?

History and settings are stored only on your device. Clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app, to remove them.
