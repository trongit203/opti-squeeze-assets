How it works

Three steps, no sign-up.

1

Pick your files

Select one or more photos or PDFs from your device.

2

Choose a quality preset

Maximum for the smallest size, Balanced for everyday use, or Lossless when quality matters most.

3

Squeeze & save

OptiSqueeze compresses each file on-device and saves the result to Downloads/OptiSqueeze.

Quality presets

One knob, tuned for different needs.

Maximum

Smallest possible file size. Best when storage or upload limits matter more than fine detail.

Balanced

The default — a solid size reduction while keeping images and documents looking sharp.

Lossless

Trims file size with no visible quality loss, for when the original look has to stay intact.
